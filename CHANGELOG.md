# Changelog

## 1.0.0

First release.

- Look down in a raid or in your hideout to see your torso, armor, rig and backpack.
- Your holstered pistol and knife show on your body.
- Your upper arms show past where the game's first person arms end.
- Your body stays clear of the camera at any FOV, standing, crouched, prone and leaning.
- No settings.
