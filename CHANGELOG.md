# Changelog

## 1.0.2

Beta.

- New setting in F12, "Body arms instead of first person arms", off by default. It hides the game's first person arms and shows your body's own arms in their place, joined to your torso. It needs Torso on.
- Your chest rig, armor and backpack stay in view without a flicker when an item goes in or out of them, such as a magazine during a reload, a stim, or the last shell loaded into a shotgun.
- Logs one line comparing the arm bones the first time it is on, for testing.

## 1.0.1

- Settings in F12: turn off the whole mod, or your torso, upper arms, body armor, chest rig, backpack, holstered pistol or knife one by one.
- Upper arms off folds your sleeves into your shoulders, so your first person arms show alone from the shoulder down.
- Your backpack is hidden by default, so a large backpack stays out of view when you look up or sprint. Turn it on in F12.

## 1.0.0

First release.

- Look down in a raid or in your hideout to see your torso, armor, rig and backpack.
- Your holstered pistol and knife show on your body.
- Your upper arms show past where the game's first person arms end.
- Your body stays clear of the camera at any FOV, standing, crouched, prone and leaning.
- No settings.
